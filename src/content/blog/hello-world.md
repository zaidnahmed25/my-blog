Welcome to my blog! This is the first post.

New technology shows up faster than most of us can keep track of. One week it's a headline, and a year later it's quietly changing how whole industries work. I want to build a deeper technical understanding of these changes as I work toward becoming a solutions architect, and I've promised myself to stay on top of where things are heading.

This blog is how I keep that promise. Writing things down forces me to actually understand them, and if it helps you keep up too, even better.

## What to expect

Each post takes one up-and-coming technology and breaks it down into three questions:

- **What is it?** A plain-language explanation, no jargon required.
- **Who does it affect?** The industries and people most likely to feel the change.
- **Where is it headed?** What it could become over the next few years.

I'll keep them easy to read, so you can follow along without a technical background.

Thanks for reading, and if there's a technology you want me to cover, [reach out](/about/).
